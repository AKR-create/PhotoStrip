Our Story & Team

About SnapStrip Studio

We are a creative team dedicated to capturing life's spontaneous moments in timeless, printable photostrips.

🎯

Our Mission

Bringing back the nostalgic magic of instant physical photobooths through modern digital web experiences, styled in warm Lapis Blue, Butter Yellow, and Oyster aesthetics.

✨

The Aesthetic

Designed with rich lapis blue bases, butter yellow highlights, and subtle oyster accents. Every photostrip generated is high-resolution, privacy-focused, and processed entirely in your browser.

🔒

100% Private

Your photos are never uploaded to remote servers. All image capturing, canvas stitching, and PNG rendering happen locally on your client device.
